LUCY: Because a guess wearing certainty's clothes can't be corrected. You'd build decisions on it. Labeled guesses are useful; disguised ones are hazards.

LUCY: Say it early, while it's still cheap. Truth delayed until after commitment isn't honesty — it's a confession scheduled for maximum damage.

LUCY: The kind lie. It compounds. Harsh truth hurts once; kind lies hurt on a schedule you don't control.
